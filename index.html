What is filtered out

  • Markets with less 24h volume than Min 24h volume (dead or thin coins). Minimum allowed is $1k; an empty box resets to $50k. Routes where any market trades under $100k/day are marked thin — your order may move the price.
  • Leveraged tokens (BTC3L, ETHUP, …).

How "You keep" is calculated

  • Every dollar figure is after all costs we know: taker fees, bid/ask spread, withdrawal fee.
  • Profit is counted only on the part of your money the best price can absorb. Unknown size = not counted.
  • Funding uses the lower of today's rate and the 3-day average, on the half of your money that is in the position. Spikes are labelled and never projected forward.
  • If nothing is worth doing, the banner says so. That is the normal state for most of the day.

Before you trade a green row

  • Size: if Max size is below your investment, the real profit will be lower.
  • Speed: triangular gaps on liquid markets last milliseconds; manual trading is usually too slow.
  • Transfers: cross-exchange needs deposits/withdrawals open on both sides; check network, fee and time.
  • Same ticker, different token: confirm the contract address on both exchanges.
  • Funding: rates change every 1–8 h and can turn negative; keep margin to avoid liquidation.
