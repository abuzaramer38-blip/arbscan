8 exchanges (Binance, Bybit, OKX, Bitget, MEXC, Gate, KuCoin, HTX) ka public data har kuch second mein uthata hai aur 3 tarah ke mauqe dhoondta hai. Koi API key nahi, koi trade nahi — sirf dekhta hai.

Hara number = trade karo?

Nahi. Pehle ye check karo:

  • Size: "Max size" sirf pehle level ka maal hai. Bara order price ko hila dega.
  • Speed: Triangular mauqe milliseconds mein khatam hote hain; jab tak haath se 3 trades karoge, mauqa ja chuka hoga.
  • Deposit/withdraw: Exchange ↔ exchange mein coin bhejna padta hai. Network, fee, aur waqt check karo. Jahan farq bohot bara ho, aksar withdraw band hota hai.
  • Same naam, alag coin: Kai exchanges par ek hi ticker alag token ka hota hai.
  • Funding: rate har 1–8 ghante badalta hai, ulta bhi ho sakta hai. Futures short par liquidation se bachne ke liye margin rakho.

Mashwara

Kuch din sirf dekho: kitne mauqe aate hain, kitni der rehte hain, kitna size hota hai. Phir faisla karo.
